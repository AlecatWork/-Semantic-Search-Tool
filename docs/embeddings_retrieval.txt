An embedding is a list of numbers that represents the meaning of a piece of text. Texts with similar meanings end up close together in vector space.
 
Cosine similarity and distance metrics measure how close two embeddings are. A smaller distance means the texts are more similar in meaning.
 
Chunking splits long documents into smaller pieces before embedding them. Smaller chunks give more precise matches, but each chunk has less surrounding context.
 
A vector database such as ChromaDB stores embeddings alongside the original text and metadata, and supports filtering results with a where clause.
